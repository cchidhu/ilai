ilai: Meals From Your Kitchen — Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026. Contact: cchidhu@gmail.com.

ilai helps a household log what it cooks and eats. This page says what the app stores, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

What we store. When you sign in with Google, we keep your Google account’s name, email address, the link to your Google profile picture and a user ID, so you can sign in again. The app doesn’t show or use the picture. In your kitchen we store what you enter: the name you go by, the kitchen’s name, its cuisines, diet and meal times, the name you give Little Idly, the meals you log (the date, the meal, the dishes, who logged or changed it, and when), dishes your kitchen adds, and invites (a code and the name of the person you invite). We store your kitchen’s time zone so “today” is right; we don’t collect your location. The Firebase SDK in the app sends a Firebase installation ID and an app-instance ID with its requests to our server. The app uses Google Play Integrity to check that requests come from the real app on a real phone.

What we don’t do. No ads. No analytics or tracking. We don’t sell or share your data. We don’t read your contacts, photos, location or other apps.

Who can see it. The people in your kitchen see your name and the kitchen’s meals. Nobody else can: the database only lets members of a kitchen read it. The data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud, Mumbai region), which processes it for us as a service provider. We would disclose data only if the law required it.

On your phone. The app keeps a copy of your kitchen on the phone so it works offline. It isn’t included in phone backups. Deleting your account clears it.

How long we keep it. As long as you’re in a kitchen. If you leave, your name stays in that kitchen’s list of people who left, next to the meals you logged. If you’re the last person to leave, the kitchen and all its meals are deleted. Invites expire after 7 days and are deleted 7 days later.

Deleting your account. In the app: Kitchen → Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in record and erases your name everywhere; meals you logged stay for the others, shown as “by a past member”. If you’re the last person in your kitchen, deleting your account deletes the kitchen and all its meals too. If you can’t open the app, email cchidhu@gmail.com from your Google account’s address and we’ll delete it within 30 days. Deleted data leaves Google’s backups on Google’s own schedule.

Age. ilai is for people 13 and over. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 is using it, email cchidhu@gmail.com and we’ll delete that account.

Changes. If this policy changes we’ll update this page and its date.
