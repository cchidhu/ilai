Delete your ilai account

  1. In the app: open ilai → Kitchen → Settings → Delete my account. Google asks you to confirm, then your account is deleted.
  2. If you can’t open the app: email cchidhu@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my ilai account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.
  3. What is deleted: your sign-in record, your name in every kitchen, and invites you made or used. What stays: meals you logged stay with the kitchen for the others, shown as “by a past member”. If you’re the last person in your kitchen, deleting your account deletes the kitchen and all its meals too, and so does leaving it (Kitchen → Settings → Leave).
  4. Privacy policy: https://cchidhu.github.io/ilai/privacy/
