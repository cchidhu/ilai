Delete your ilai account

  1. In the app: Kitchen → Settings → Delete my account. If you have no kitchen (you never set one up, or you just left one), it’s at the bottom of the Start screen and of the screen after leaving. You confirm with the account you signed in with: Google or Apple. If you signed in with Apple, ilai also revokes its Sign in with Apple access. Then your account is deleted.
  2. If you can’t open the app: email planmealwithilai@gmail.com from the email address of the Google or Apple account you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my ilai account”. If you hide your email with Apple, say so and tell us the name you go by in your kitchen. We’ll delete it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.
  3. What is deleted: your sign-in record, your name in every kitchen, the invites you used, and the open invites you made. What stays: meals you logged stay with the kitchen for the others, shown as “by a past member”. An invite you made that someone else used stays until it is deleted automatically, up to 14 days after it was made; it holds your user ID but not your name. Usage and crash reports already sent are kept for at most 14 months (crash reports 90 days) without your name or email; deleting your account clears your user ID from new ones. A short marker with your user ID, so a phone still signed in can’t put you back in a kitchen, is deleted automatically a day or two later. If you’re the last person in your kitchen, deleting your account deletes the kitchen and all its meals too, and so does leaving it (Kitchen → Settings → Leave).
  4. Privacy policy: https://cchidhu.github.io/ilai/privacy/
